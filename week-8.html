After consultation, I read The Silent Language by Edward T. Hall

This book is for cross-referencing between lunar and Gregorian calendars, used mainly for tracing one's parents' birthdays or grandparents' anniversaries. "You can now celebrate your birthday twice each year, once each according to the two calendars!" The author noted this will not happen every year, because every nineteen years the date coincides, or will not differ by more than 3 days.
